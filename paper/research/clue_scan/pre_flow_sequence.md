## THE HONEST READING (baseline 0.618)

**1. The price read alone is a WEAK seat.** range-bottom + fresh
20d low WITHOUT any flow/effort confirmation: 0.638-0.643
(n=757/661) - about +2.5pts over baseline. What the eye sees as an
obvious bottom is, at the daily grain, barely better than a coin
from this universe's own 0.618. The chart alone is not the edge -
it is the SEAT, not the signal.

**2. Effort reads beat price reads BEFORE the flow confirms.**
ABSORPTION (effort>result within 5d at the seat): 0.662 (n=71);
COIL alone adds nothing (0.620, n=250); CLIMAX volume adds nothing
(0.615, n=109); coil+absorption 0.727 (n=22 - THIN, recorded not
claimed). The best measurable PRE-flow marker is your effort-vs-
result: absorption is the one that beats both price-alone and
coil/climax before orderflow says anything.

**3. The flow confirms LATE - and that is the pre-flow window.**
Median timing from the seat: washout flow turns on +17 days later;
the XB deep zone +20.5 days; the first big move arrives +29 days.
So the sequence is PRICE SEAT -> (effort markers) -> FLOW
CONFIRMATION ~2.5 weeks AFTER the seat -> the measured move ~4
weeks after the seat. The pre-flow window is real and about 2-3
weeks wide: within it, absorption (0.66) is the best read we have
measured; outside it, the flow itself (XB_old_low 0.753 / flagship
0.731) is the confirmation that arrives before the move.

**The answer to 'what is the best before orderflow':** at the daily
grain - the SEAT (price extreme + fresh low, weak alone) read
TOGETHER with EFFORT (absorption: volume effort where the move
should have been and was not). Coil marks the seat's patience,
not its direction; climax marks nothing. The flow's own
confirmation follows ~2.5 weeks behind the seat and carries the
bigger chips; the trade timing question (enter at the pre-flow
absorption read vs wait for the washout confirm) is now a framed
MEASURED trade-off, not a guess: waiting costs ~2.5 weeks of
exposure to get ~+7pts of reliability (0.75 vs 0.66-0.64).
