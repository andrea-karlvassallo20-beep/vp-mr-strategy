## THE HONEST READING (cells with healthy n; full table in the CSV)

Baseline all nights: 0.559 (h1) / 0.591 (h2).

**1. The human read SURVIVES discretization - and the shape adds
beyond the state alone.** At range bottoms (range_pos<=0.20, last leg
UP): U-base up-legs (back-loaded, the move builds late) 0.654
(n=8,161) vs V-spike 0.634 and even 0.634 - and the U-base x WASHOUT
flow cell reads **0.731 (n=806)**, the strongest bottom formation
measured by the program, above both the bare fresh-low (0.578) and
the flow states alone (session-40 ext_low 0.686). The U the owner
draws at lows, read WITH the flow axis, is measured signal.

**2. At tops the read INVERTS by shape - the chart reader's most
actionable asymmetry.** At range tops (range_pos>=0.80, last leg
DOWN): the washed U-dome rollover (back-loaded down leg - slow
distribution) reads **0.331 (n=987)**: the first big move resolves
DOWN two times in three. The V-spike crash at highs reads 0.542
(n=1,238) - NO edge: V tops that "must come down" often bounce
first; blind-shorting spikes is the natural human error and the
numbers refuse to license it. What must come down is the WASHED
rollover, not the crash.

**3. Sequentiality (honest negative):** the up-then-down 2-gram at
tops carries no measurable content beyond the 1-gram down-leg shape
at this grain (identical n; identical reading) - the ORDER adds
nothing once the completed shape is known. Deeper sequence tests
(3-grams, leg-size order) remain unmeasured: NEW declarations, not
this session's.

**4. Flow relation in space and time (the owner's "position in space
and their relation in time"):** washout flow lifts EVERY bottom shape
(0.689-0.731 over flowmid counterparts 0.656-0.664); at tops the
euphoric cells are too thin for standalone claims (n=17-76) - left
unclaimed, no multiplicity spent.

All in-sample descriptive; every candidate owes the same W2;
nothing armed; nothing trades live.
