rewritten in place. The sequence-gap criterion is timestamp-independent and
was unaffected.

**Gate methodology notes (all recorded, none weakened):**
- Sequence continuity is checked once per MESSAGE (U/u/pu), not per level-row.
- The book is seeded/reset from `depthSnapshot` at every feed (re)connect
  (the Binance-documented procedure); Tardis emits a fresh snapshot at each
  reconnect and the diff stream restarts from it. This reset semantics was
  the difference between 97% and 100% mid agreement on 2026-10-01.
- Comparison clock: the `book_snapshot_25` ground truth is constructed on
  Tardis local-timestamp ordering, so book states are compared on the
  common local clock (`--align local`); exchange-clock alignment was
  measured at 97% mid / 54% depth — pure feed-latency skew, not state error.
- Depth comparison is capped at the 25-level snapshot's coverage boundary
  (full-depth reconstruction vs level-limited ground truth, equal footing).

## Output Contract — Claim L0-Gate

**CLAIM:** Incremental L2 order-book replay on BTCUSDT USDS-M perpetual
futures reconstructs mid-price within 1 tick and cumulative depth within
10 bps of mid at ≥ 99% agreement against independent exchange snapshots
across 100 random timestamps with a sequence gap rate < 0.01%.

**MECHANISM:** Queue-aware liquidity metrics require lossless causal
sequencing of order additions, modifications, and cancellations; any
unlogged sequence break corrupts downstream state reconstruction.

**TEST:** Replay of raw sequence-numbered @depth diffs (U, u, pu) with
depthSnapshot seeding/reset at every feed (re)connect, per-day gap logging
to gaps.log with INVALIDATED-until-resync semantics, and 100-random-
timestamp audits against book_snapshot_25 (2026-10-01) / the replay's own
depthSnapshot messages (other days).

**KILL LINE:** Sequence gap rate ≥ 0.01%, mid-price agreement < 99%, or
10-bps depth agreement < 99% on any day halts execution (exit 1, 'GATE
FAILED') and blocks all downstream layers.
