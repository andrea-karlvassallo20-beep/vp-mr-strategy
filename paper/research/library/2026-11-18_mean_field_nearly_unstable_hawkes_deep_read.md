## The paper in one paragraph

Two generalizations of the nearly-unstable Hawkes limit theory:
(i) scaling limits under only the asymptotic criticality condition
||phi_n||_L1 -> 1 (dropping Jaisson-Rosenbaum's proportional-
kernel assumption phi_n = a_n*phi), showing the rescaled intensity
necessarily converges to stochastic Volterra diffusions of affine
type — the CIR process when the kernel has finite first moment
(the Heston micro-foundation), and rough/fractional kernels with
power-law tails (alpha in (1/2, 1)) otherwise, linking Hawkes
criticality to rough volatility (PDF pp. 1-4); and (ii) a
propagation-of-chaos result for mean-field Hawkes particle systems
(n particles sharing a common renormalized intensity): the
particles become ASYMPTOTICALLY INDEPENDENT inhomogeneous Poisson
processes driven by one common Volterra intensity field, with
THREE distinct limiting regimes depending on the interplay of n
and (1 - ||phi_n||)^2 (PDF pp. 3-4).

criticality sequence — a model property, never a guarantee that any
real window sits near criticality; the mean-field regime assumes

rough-volatility links are inherited from the Jaisson-Rosenbaum
