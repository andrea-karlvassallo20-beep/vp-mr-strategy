## The paper in one paragraph

The paper constructs the missing microstructure for the rough
Hawkes-Heston model WITH common price-volatility jumps: a Poisson-
embedded marked Hawkes order-flow model in which ORDINARY arrivals
generate rough continuous volatility and leverage through the
nearly-unstable heavy-tailed Hawkes mechanism (the El-Euch/
Rosenbaum route: near-critical renewal resolvent converges to a
Mittag-Leffler kernel, rewritten in Riemann-Liouville fractional
form), while RARE MARKED arrivals represent common shock events
producing simultaneous downward log-price jumps (-Lambda*z) and
positive volatility excitation through one compensated jump
measure (PDF pp. 1-3). Under near-critical scaling and reduced-
form admissibility conditions, the full rescaled price/variance/
jump system converges along the whole sequence to the unique
canonical rough Hawkes-Heston weak solution; the limiting
coefficients are explicit in the microscopic parameters, and the
effective Volterra drift is set by the BALANCE between the near-
critical Hawkes gap and the predictable mean of common-jump
feedback — negative within the subcritical regime (PDF pp. 1-2).
The leverage correlation is generated by a martingale-BALANCED
ASYMMETRY of sign-specific arrival rates and tick sizes (PDF
abstract). Numerical experiments verify convergence.
