What died, what survived

Died: every AVFI entry rule (27 variants, jointly and individually), the state-transition system, the bear-capitulation timer (one episode, extinct states since 2010), every parameter "plateau".

Survived: a one-line trend rule (12-week momentum), and now, for the first time, a job for the indicator itself: as a risk throttle on position size, not as an entry timer.

The indicator was a slow regime descriptor all along. The edge, such as it is, is generic trend. The sophistication budget is best spent on sizing, not entries.

Lessons learned

01

The indicator was the least important part

Months of Pine craft, 27 variants, zero passes. A one-line trend rule beat all of them. Craft is not edge.

02

Controls or it did not happen

The state system scored 0.42 in validation and looked fine until matched random entries scored 0.465 on the same window. Every edge is now judged against random-entry and buy-hold anchors.

03

One-episode edges are seductive

Bear-capitulation: +0.23 sigma next week, p = 0.03, textbook. All 90 of its weeks were one 2008-2010 episode, and the state has been extinct since 2010. Significance from one regime is history, not edge.

04

Elegant conditions can be vacuous

The pre-registered |sp_z|>=1 filter produced zero trades ever, because transitions happen exactly when the spread is near zero. Test that a rule can trade before testing whether it wins.

05

Costs were not the enemy

Tripling slippage moved Sharpe by under 0.012. Absence of edge was the enemy. Cost paranoia is misplaced at this holding period.

06

Parameter sensitivity usually hides a slowness artifact

The H5 "plateau" decomposed into pure slow-EMA drift. When a parameter works only at extreme values, find the simple thing it proxies.

07

Data discipline precedes strategy discipline

Repairs logged one by one, cross-checks against the cash index, single-source risk quantified before any rule ran. Bad data produces confident nonsense.

08

Reproducibility is insurance

Six environment wipes, six identical rebuilds, same seed, same numbers. A result that cannot survive a rebuild was never a result.

09

Power analysis before the irreversible decision

The holdout opens once. Before touching it: the bar is ~0.75 observed Sharpe over 307 weeks, so a true 0.57 edge passes only 43% of the time. Knowing the odds first is the difference between testing and gambling.

10

Entries got the attention; sizing was the untouched lever

The first sizing change in the whole programme improved drawdown by a third. The next gains here come from how much, not when.

The decision point

The 2021+ holdout is sealed and opens exactly once. The battery as registered tests rule set T; the proposed Amendment 2 adds rule set U3 to the same single open (family max-t adjusted). Three options: go (verdict now, coin-flip confirmation odds, a pass is strong, a fail closes the registered weekly claims), wait (two more years buys 15 power points), veto (holdout sealed forever, forward paper testing only).
