The programme's honesty, made visible. Every claim in the registry carries its pre-declared kill line, its current status, and the evidence that put it there. Nothing is retired quietly; nothing is promoted without its kill line written first. Statuses: tested-fail (kill line fired, mechanism named), tested-pass (survived its kill lines out-of-sample), blocked-on-data (cannot be honestly tested until the data arrives), open (running, window not closed), pending decision (owner's call, memo written).

The epistemic contract

Every claim gets its kill line in writing before it is tested. Every kill line that fires gets its mechanism named in the record. Nothing is re-tuned on the discovery panel; nothing is validated on data that already exists; nothing directional is claimed while the 0.505 boundary stands; and — added 2026-11-16, after the math verification found five defects in our own estimators — no threshold is frozen before the estimator that will measure it is verified against its source or an independent oracle. The ledger is the honesty — updated with every PR, never quietly.
