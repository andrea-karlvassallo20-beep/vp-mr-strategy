## 1. What autonomy is granted, and what it is not

Granted: selection and invention of the next unit of null-time work. Which
paper is read, when a synthesis pass runs, what probe is proposed, what gap
is named — these become session decisions, made against a written goal
state, recorded with reasons, and contestable in the log.

NOT granted, unchanged, owner-only (the carve-outs):

- Arming any claim, test, or spec; the armed list stays owner-fired.
- Signing, amending, or withdrawing any dated amendment.
- Spending data, budget, or sealed windows (the L2 purchase, the
  cross-market holdouts, the watchlist windows).
- Moving any kill line, frozen constant, or frozen default.
- Approving sharpening candidates: they queue as PENDING OWNER
  RATIFICATION, exactly as before.

Any product of autonomy that falls in these classes routes to
`docs/DECISIONS_PENDING.md` (if a sharpening) or to the log (everything
else) and is inert until the owner acts.

## 3. The director policy (how a session chooses; reconstructed from the
landing session's log entry, plus the owner's rotation guidance)

At session start, after re-reading the live main tail and the branch/PR set
(the main-wins race protocol is unchanged):

1. **Default:** if the standing deep-read ritual has a readable UNREAD
   head, the session may run it, exactly as codified. Nothing is revoked.
2. **Cadence rule:** if five or more deep-read sessions have landed since
   the current goal-state edition, the session runs the synthesis pass
   FIRST — as its own session, or before its read; the pass then produces
   the next edition before further selection.
3. **Rotation guidance (the owner's dated instruction):** the deep-read
   default is the floor, not the target. Work type should rotate across
   the fronts: when a non-deep-read front is lawfully executable (the
   misses-curriculum protocol draft, the honesty pass, a probe proposal,
   an infrastructure repair), a session may and should take it; the
   session-state note records the choice and the reason against the goal
   state.
4. **Overrides:** any deviation from the above is recorded with a reason
   tied to the written backlog; unreasoned overrides are protocol
   violations, logged as such.
5. **Fail-safe:** if this protocol file is unreachable or inconsistent,
   the session falls back to the fixed ritual and flags the file.

## 5. The weekly directorate (dated owner instruction; convention, not a
session power)

One short sitting on the owner's calendar — the objective is roughly ten
minutes — where the current front ranking and the pending-queue shortlist
are presented and the owner batch-ratifies or kills proposals in one
decision sitting. Sessions never schedule, fire, or preempt it; they keep
the ranking ready and current so any sitting can happen without
preparation. The one-sitting batch replaces dozens of per-item gates;
nothing outside it changes status: arming, amendment signatures,
kill-line moves, sharpening approvals and every other carve-out remain
owner-only, and the ratification queue's zero-armed state holds until the
owner acts.
