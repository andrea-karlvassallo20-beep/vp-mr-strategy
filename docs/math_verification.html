The Math, Verified.

The plain-language record of how the two pieces of public math the programme's L2 layer stands on — order flow imbalance and the Hawkes process — were verified against their published sources and an independent implementation, and what that verification found hiding in our own code. The full technical record: paper/research/math_verification_2026-11-16.md; the re-runnable evidence: src/verify_math_library.py.

The conclusion, in one line

The math is now verified end-to-end — and verification earned its keep: five defects were found and fixed in our own implementations before any of it touched a claim.

The formula is authoritative, not the code. That sentence is the method.

1 · Why verify public math at all

The mathematics is public: high-frequency traders run Hawkes processes and order flow imbalance in milliseconds, off papers anyone can download. Retail traders do not know it exists. The programme's bet — hypothesis C8 — is that the edge is the combination: this public math, on a frontier market's limit order book, at a macro horizon (4h/24h) where nobody runs it, with kill lines written before the data exists. But a combination built on a mis-copied formula is not an edge; it is a bug wearing a thesis. So before anything else: extract the exact formulas from the sources, and cross-check our implementations against an independent library. Nothing below tests a claim. It makes the claims possible to test honestly.

2 · The exact formula, from the LaTeX source

The order flow imbalance of Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov (The Price Impact of Order Book Events, arXiv:1011.6402v3) is not the paraphrase found in most blog posts. The paper's own source defines the contribution of the n-th book event to the queues as:

en = 1{PBn ≥ PBn-1} · qBn − 1{PBn ≤ PBn-1} · qBn-1
     − 1{PAn ≤ PAn-1} · qAn + 1{PAn ≥ PAn-1} · qAn-1

Two properties matter, and both were wrong in our code. First, the indicators are non-strict (≥, ≤): when a price does not move, both fire and the event contributes the queue change — added liquidity positive, removed negative. Same-price events are the majority of book events. Second, when a price does move, the contribution is the full level size (the whole new queue at the improved price; the whole withdrawn queue at the dropped one), not the small difference across levels. Our implementation was compared event-by-event against a literal transcription of this formula on 25 randomized books × 2,000 events: maximum difference after the fix, 0.0.

4 · The cross-check against tick

tick is the open-source point-process library maintained by the X-Data initiative. The two libraries speak different parameterizations of the same model — ours: kernel α·e-βt; tick's: kernel G·β·e-βt, where its "adjacency" is the branching matrix — and the verification documents the bridge explicitly. Three checks, each on exact simulations with known planted truth:

CheckMethodResult
Likelihood exactnessOur O(N) recursion vs an independent O(N²) oracle written straight from the intensity definition, on tick-simulated datamax relative difference 1.6×10-14
2D branching recoveryOur MLE and tick's estimator on tick-simulated 2D data, vs the planted ρ(G)ours within 0.086, tick within 0.033
1D branching (Filimonov–Sornette)The cheatsheet's fit — executed verbatim from the markdown — vs tick on 1D simulationsboth within 0.04 of the planted branching ratio

One honest limitation is recorded rather than papered over: tick 0.8.0.2's own log-likelihood model returns values inconsistent with its documentation (probed on handcrafted Poisson data), so the sharp likelihood check runs against the independent brute-force oracle instead — tick enters through its simulator and its estimator, where it behaves exactly as it should.

5 · Why D5 mattered most

The C8 trigger fires when the Hawkes branching ratio exceeds 0.8 — the market going critical, most trades begotten by other trades. The old metric overstated the branching ratio by the factor β = 2.0. Read that again with the trigger in mind: every ordinary market with a true branching ratio of 0.4 would have registered as 0.8. The trigger would have fired constantly, the hypothesis would have been "tested" against noise, and the failure would have been blamed on the idea — not the arithmetic. The 0.8 threshold was frozen only after this fix, on the true ρ(G). This is why verification precedes registration, and why both precede data.

6 · What this page cannot tell you

Verified math is not a verified edge. The formula being flawless says nothing about whether the combination works: C8 remains declared, untested, blocked-on-data, with eleven kill lines armed. Direction remains refused — the endpoint is a WHEN instrument, sized by the posterior on MOVE, not UP. And no amount of correct arithmetic substitutes for the L2 data itself: the gate, the base layer (C1-L2), then the synthesis. What this page records is narrower and prior to all of that: when the data finally arrives, the math it will be measured with is now provably the math the papers actually wrote.
