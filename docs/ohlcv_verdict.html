How the 30-minute study was done · what the numbers said · why the answer is a victory

The OHLCV Verdict.

The plain-language record of the programme's grandest falsification: how a six-year, 78-asset, 5.3-million-row dataset was studied, and how the conclusion — OHLCV-derived compression is statistically worthless for predicting forward returns — was arrived at without ever training a model.

The conclusion, in one line

All OHLCV-derived edges are dead. The program now rests entirely on the L2 order book data.

Owner-confirmed, 2026-11-16. The full technical record: paper/research/phase1_daily/c7_30m_test_result.md; the executive summary: docs/EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md.

1 · The data

A 1.27GB parquet: 5,308,089 rows × 43 columns, 78 crypto perpetuals, six years of 30-minute bars (2020-07 → 2026-06). Momentum, reversal, volatility, liquidity (Amihud, ADV), betas, cross-asset lead-lag vs BTC/ETH/SOL — every feature derived from OHLCV (open-high-low-close-volume). No order book. No queue. No cancellations.

The label: y_hold = the 4-hour forward return, rank-normalized cross-sectionally per era — recovered empirically from the panel's own prices (per-era Spearman 0.71–0.87 against the computed 4h forward return) and confirmed by the owner. responder, the second label, is the next-30-minute return — a different question, kept out of this test.

2 · How it was studied — five steps, in order

Step 1: the intake audit

Before anything touched the data: schema, coverage, integrity. Fully dense (zero nulls), timestamps in microseconds, no missing calendar days. One open item — the label definition — was resolved empirically rather than guessed.

Step 2: the leakage audit

For 60 eras spread across the whole file, every feature's cross-sectional correlation with the label. No leaks — the strongest feature sat at |ρ| = 0.196, far below the 0.80 leak line. But the audit found something just as important: the six "volatility" features are one factor wearing six names — σ20 vs f_vol_21: 0.99; vs realized_vol_30: 0.94; vs f_range_21: 0.92. "Quietness" is a single axis, not six independent clues.

Step 3: pre-registration — the rules locked before the answer existed

Five kill lines in writing, and a frozen split manifest: train 2020–2023, validation 2024–2025, test 2025–2026 (untouched to this day). Purge at every boundary for the 4-hour label horizon; a 24-hour embargo because crypto regimes shift. This is what makes the verdict trustworthy: no rule was invented after seeing the result.

Step 4: the test — and the control group that made it honest

The question was never "does quietness predict?" — quietness is the baseline. The question was: does the PRIMED state — quiet AND at the bottom of its 63-day range, the "coil" — predict MORE than quietness alone? Every era split its assets into two groups:

groupdefinitionper era (mean)
Primedσ20 in the bottom 33% AND momentum in the bottom 35% — the coiled state9.0 assets
Controlσ20 in the bottom 33% but NOT at the range bottom — quiet, not coiled12.5 assets

If the coil adds anything real, Primed must beat Control. The comparison isolates the costume from the wearer.

3 · How "worthless" was arrived at

The vol-tautology kill line fired: the Primed state does not outperform the Quiet-Not-Primed control. And this is the third independent death of the compression family:

testresolutionhow it died
C1 — compression duration-dependencedaily bars, BTC & ETH, 2021–2026vol-tautology: after conditioning on realized volatility alone, the compression flag's hazard-ratio CI included 1.0 in 4/4 cells; a block-temporal placebo reproduced the entire effect
The watchlist — four primed coils (KAVA, HOLO, GENIUS, SOL)daily, forward test (window open)all four broke downward within days; the coil fired without promising direction — observational, not claimed
C7-30m — compression priming30-minute bars, 78 assets, 6 years, 16,402 erasvol-tautology: Primed − Control CI includes zero; 50.4% of eras positive — a coin flip

Three different resolutions, three different universes, the same verdict: compression measured from OHLCV is volatility wearing a costume. The C1 death was not a resolution artifact — it reproduced everywhere it was re-asked. The coil identifies movement; it does not identify rank, sign, or 4-hour edge.

4 · Why this is a victory

Because the programme's product is the elimination of noise. Every dead claim is a question you never have to wonder about again — and this one was the programme's founding intuition, tested honestly at three resolutions and buried with the mechanism named. What remains is not "promising"; it is untested: the L2 order-book family (C1-L2, C5, C6, C7-panel), where compression would be measured from the book itself — resting liquidity aging, asks pulling, queues draining — the things candles cannot see. The Tesla-day anatomy (a +10% hour with near-balanced taker flow; the book did the work) is the observation that keeps that family worth gating.

5 · What this page cannot tell you

That the L2 family will survive. It is untested, not alive — it faces its own armed kill lines the day the order-book data passes the gate. And nothing anywhere in this record touches direction: the 0.505 boundary [0.497, 0.513] is unbroken, and the endpoint remains a WHEN instrument, sized by the posterior on MOVE, not UP.
