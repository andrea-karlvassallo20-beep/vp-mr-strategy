Order-flow microstructure · engine demonstration

Compound Hawkes Order Flow · a 12-dimensional limit order book

A self-exciting and self-inhibiting point process drives a six-queue top-of-book simulator: limit, cancel and market orders at the touch, one tick deep, and inside the spread, on both sides. Order sizes are compound marks with round-number spikes. The spread can never fall below one tick, by construction. Nothing here is market data.

Day 0 · mid price and spread

Mid price in ticks (left) with the spread in ticks (right), one point per second. The U-shape of activity lifts volatility at the open and into the close. Queue-depletion events widen the spread one tick at a time; in-spread limit orders reclaim it.

Day 0 · liquidity book map

Stacked resting liquidity at the four visible queues, sampled every 5 seconds: bid deep and bid top (blues, mirrored) versus ask top and ask deep (reds). The mirror construction is stylistic; the queues are the engine's own book state, conserved through every event by tested invariants.

Day 0 · the twelve event streams

Counts per 30 seconds for each of the twelve dimensions. Market order dimensions show bursts exactly when the memory of the process says they should; cancel and in-spread dimensions respond with their own kernels; the deep levels breathe more slowly.

Endogenous share of intensity · day 0

Fraction of instantaneous total intensity attributed to excitation from past events rather than the exogenous baseline, sampled every 30 seconds. Attribution is proportional and declared; under the Poisson control this share is exactly zero ().

Kernel-norm matrix M (12 × 12)

Rows excite columns. Red is catalysing, blue is inhibitory; the diagonal carries self-excitation. The engine accepts signed kernels and floors total intensity at zero, so inhibition is a live mechanism here, not decoration. Spectral radius is disclosed on every build; nothing is silently rescaled.

Signature plot · volatility versus sampling scale

Realised volatility of mid-price changes at sampling scales from 1 s to 300 s, Hawkes day against the Poisson control (identical book, sizes, and time-of-day conditioning, zero memory). Clustering of order flow separates the two curves exactly the way microstructure folklore says it should. This is a property of the synthetic engine.

Absolute-return autocorrelation · day 0

Autocorrelation of absolute one-second mid-price changes, lags 1-120 s. The Hawkes process keeps a long positive memory in volatility; the Poisson control is flat. Volatility clustering is visible without a single fitted parameter: it is planted by the kernels and read back by the statistic.

Inter-arrival times versus the memoryless benchmark

Log-scale histograms of event gaps for three dimensions, with the exponential distribution at the same rate (dashed) as the reference a pure Poisson process would produce. The left tails (many more very short gaps than exponential) are the excitation showing through; the in-spread dimension is additionally spread-state gated.

Order-size distributions · compound marks

Empirical size distributions per order class (log scale). The Dirac masses at round numbers 1, 10, 50, 100, 200 and 500 shares are planted deliberately, mixing trader preference into a geometric body. Market orders run larger than limit orders; cancels track the queues they consume.

Spread distribution · day 0

Time spent at each spread in ticks, one second resolution. The one-tick spread is unreachable by construction: the engine asserts spread ≥ 1 tick after every single event, and that invariant is under test.

Time-of-day factors f(Qt)

The separable U-shape multipliers per dimension: thirteen 30-minute bins, each row normalised to mean 1. Every intensity in the engine carries this deterministic clock. Deep-read 19 and 20 of the research programme recorded why any branching-style reading must disclose and separate this component; this engine keeps it separable by construction.

Stability disclosure · the no-silent-capping rule, as code

The stationarity condition for a d-dimensional Hawkes intensity system is that the spectral radius of the kernel-norm matrix stays below one divided by the largest time-of-day factor. Simulators in the literature occasionally violate the fitted constraint and quietly multiply the kernels back under it. This engine does the opposite of quiet: it refuses an unstable matrix on request, or rescales to a declared target and returns a disclosure record with the raw eigenvalue, the scaled eigenvalue, and the exact factor applied.
