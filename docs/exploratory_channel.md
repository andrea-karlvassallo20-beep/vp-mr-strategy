# The exploratory channel (ratified 2026-11-18)

Standing protocol for exploratory work in the null-time programme. Ratified
by the owner's word in chat on 2026-11-18 ("Go for it while we wait for l2"),
adopting the proposal as stated: give serendipity a declared channel instead
of making it smuggle. The owner may revoke or amend this channel at any
time; its existence is logged in `paper/research/null_time_programme.md`
(entry ## 25).

## Purpose

Cross-domain imports and accidents have a documented history in this field
(Hawkes processes from seismology, random-matrix theory from nuclear physics,
multifractal cascades from turbulence). The imports that survived arrived as
mechanisms with constraints attached. This channel exists so that such
imports are explored openly, logged, and graded, instead of arriving as
unlogged decoration or being silently foreclosed.

## Scope and limits

- Up to one exploratory block per null-time session, at the session's
  discretion, announced at the top of the log entry when used. Sessions that
  run the standing library deep-read also keep their one-paper-per-session
  rule; the exploratory block is additional to, never instead of, evidence
  discipline.
- A probe may read anything (no triage restriction), derive anything, and
  propose anything. Anchors must be named honestly: source papers and pages,
  or "own derivation".
- A probe may NOT: measure anything (data runs stay frozen behind the
  standing gates and must use the registered pipeline once open), arm
  anything, touch any kill line, or carry any evidentiary weight.

## Logging rule

- Every probe gets a numbered note under `paper/research/exploratory/`
  (E1, E2, ...), dated same as its log entry.
- The log entry tags exploratory content with an [exploratory] tag.
- The multiple-testing discipline counts every probe. Nothing ever glimpsed
  exploratorily may be cited as confirmatory support for any program claim;
  an exploratory idea earns evidentiary standing only by surviving a test that
  was registered AFTER the exploration ended. An accident gets one chance to
  matter, and only through the front gate.

## Output gate

- A probe that finds something promising writes a candidate (sharpening,
  claim, or engineering validation) through the normal PENDING OWNER
  RATIFICATION gate. The owner's word is the only trigger. A probe can never
  arm anything by itself.
- Negative results are logged identically to the deep-read standard: honest
  grade, caveats, no silent burial.
- An exploratory idea is ammunition, not evidence.
