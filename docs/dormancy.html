3 · What this page cannot tell you

Whether any quiet series will move, which way, or when. The heat map is a watchlist, not a forecast. The forward test on the 2026-11-12 watchlist (KAVA, HOLO, GENIUS, SOL) already showed the coil fires without promising direction — three of four broke downward within two days.
