The Night-Before Sauce Move.

"No chart contains the move it precedes. It contains the state — and the state is what C7 measured at +7.9 points over matched quiet."
— the census's sharpest lesson, now a complete list.

This page is the complete registry of every factor that can be derived from the prior day (the last quiet day) to detect significant moves: what is already measured, what is computable from data already in the database, what is plausibly predictive but blocked, and — stated as loudly as the rest — what no prior day can ever tell you. The machine-readable registry lives at paper/research/night_before_criteria.md; the live panel at strategy/avfi_v5_night_before.pine computes every single-symbol factor on the chart. Descriptive only; multiplicity binds; C7 sealed.

The composite — the forgotten-and-pinned signature

One boolean, five factors: range position ≤ 0.25 AND vol percentile ≤ 30 AND coil days ≥ 10 AND volume ratio < 1 AND year trend < 0. Low in range, quiet, coiled, dry, down on the year — the state the census found before crypto's up-moves, shaded purple in the Pine panel and labeled STATE, not a signal. Its conditional hazard versus matched controls has not been measured; that measurement, not this list, is what would make it detective rather than descriptive.

What no prior day can ever tell you

Four refusals, on the record: which specific asset (base rates are per-class fractions), when to the day (the census windows are 30 days), how big (nothing in the night-before panel scales with the outcome), and direction from the same state — the up and down signatures are near-mirror images of one state; the state conditions hazard, not sign.

The only path from factor to belief

Identical for every row above: base rate from the census → control rate from matched non-movers that shared the state → causal rule declared in advance → dual-split gate (beat the current parameters on BOTH out-of-sample windows, scored at the worse one) → forward log, pre-logged and read when the window closes, whatever it says. The four pre-logged candidates (KAVA, HOLO, GENIUS, SOL; window closing 2027-01-11) are the first live pass. A registry lists what can be asked — the gates decide what is true.
