The hypothesis

Markets are a matching engine plus participants managing inventory plus informed flow. The transaction price oscillates around a latent efficient price that evolves in trading time, not calendar time. Structure exists, but it is statistical, not geometric. Six unconditional laws hold; the central theorem forbids what no public theory can honestly sell:

The certainty-timing-direction triangle: a rule that identified an event as certain, timed, and directional would be traded until it stopped working — so it cannot exist publicly. CERTAINTY lives at the level of the distribution, PROBABILITY at the level of the event, HAZARD at the level of timing. Elliott Wave claimed all three and could never be wrong; this framework separates them so every claim can be scored — and can fail in public, which is the only way anything in markets can ever be right.

1. Volatility persists, then mean-reverts
High vol weeks follow high vol weeks; compressed vol expands. This is the clock itself.
2. Tails are heavy
Return distributions are non-Gaussian at every horizon. All thresholds live in sigma units, never in fixed points.
3. Autocorrelation has horizon structure
Momentum at medium horizons, reversal at long ones, near-zero at short ones. The directional prior - valid only inside trend states.
4. Impact is concave in volume
Big moves require big flow, and flow leaves footprints. A move without volume is a suspect move.
5. Events self-excite
Price changes cluster in time, mostly endogenous. Hazard is state-dependent: timing forecasts are conditional distributions, never constants.
6. Every exploitable regularity decays
The market adapts to whoever uses it. Every rule carries a forward calibration score and a decay watch. Nothing is forever, by law.

Four measured states replace wave counting — COMPRESSION (the event-prone state), TREND, EXPANSION, STRESS — and the primary clock is volatility: sigma_w, its tercile, and the frozen conditional tables give the hazard of a significant event (≥ 2.5σw within 4 weeks) at every decision close. The full charter lives in the repository as KNOWLEDGE_SMH.md.

Strictly causal, stated in advance, scored against outcomes every night — and never a direction, per the central theorem. This is the future event we see and can explain.
