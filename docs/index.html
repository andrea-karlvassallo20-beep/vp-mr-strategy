How this works

Every trading day after the US market close, this system fetches real bars for SPY, QQQ, IWM, XLF and XLE, runs its frozen strategy, audits every decision (SPI), writes a performance report, runs an edge-seeking research pass, re-evaluates its own promotion gate, and republishes this page. It never changes its own parameters — proposals are committed for human review.
