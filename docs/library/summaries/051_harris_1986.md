# 051 — A Theory of Intraday Patterns: Volume and Price Variability (Harris, 1986)

**Venue:** Review of Financial Studies | **Category:** Microstructure & order flow
**Status:** Synthesized from LLM internal knowledge (Paywalled paper).

## Summary
Explains the intraday U/J-shape of volume and volatility as the
rational concentration of information-motivated trading around
the open and close: traders with information prefer to trade when
liquidity is deep and when other informed traders are active,
producing self-reinforcing clustering at session boundaries.

## The math that matters
Strategic timing: expected profit of trading at time $t$ rises
with the probability that counterparties are uninformed and with
price informativeness dynamics; equilibrium produces
$\lambda(t)$-shaped arrival intensities with peaks at open/close.

## Why it matters for the L2 project
Intraday seasonality is a confound for every 30m-bar feature: a
"quiet" era measured across different times of day is a different
object. C8's micro features must either be computed in the volume
clock (048) or seasonality-adjusted before cross-era comparison.
