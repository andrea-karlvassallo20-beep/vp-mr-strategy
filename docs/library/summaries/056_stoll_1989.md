# 056 — The Components of the Bid-Ask Spread: A General Approach (Stoll, 1989)

**Venue:** Journal of Finance | **Category:** Microstructure & order flow
**Status:** Synthesized from LLM internal knowledge (Paywalled paper).

## Summary
Decomposes the spread into order-processing, inventory, and
adverse-selection components using only the covariance structure
of quotes and returns — no micro data on trades or quotes
required. Finds the majority of the spread is compensation for
adverse selection and inventory, not processing costs.

## The math that matters
Effective/relative spread $S$ relates to the serial covariance of
quote-midpoint returns: the transitory component implies
$\text{Cov}(\Delta M_t, \Delta M_{t+1}) < 0$, and the proportions
$\pi_{ad} + \pi_i + \pi_p = 1$ are identified from the
first-order autocovariance and the spread:
$\frac{\text{Cov}(\Delta M_t, \Delta M_{t+1})}{\text{Var}} =
-\frac{S^2}{8}$-type moment conditions.

## Why it matters for the L2 project
A zero-instrumentation check on the perps books: the C8 gate can
verify that observed spreads are consistent with the covariance
implied transitory/permanent split — a sanity check on data
quality and on feature construction.
