## Summary
A bibliometric review of the Markov-switching literature: growth
trends, application domains (economics, finance, engineering),
methodological clusters (MS-AR, MS-VAR, MS-GARCH, time-varying
transitions), and co-citation structure. Its value to the project
is navigational: it maps which regime-switching variants are
established and which application areas are saturated.

## The math that matters
None new — the review catalogs the Hamilton filter family (see
021, 022, 023) rather than deriving.

## Why it matters for the L2 project
Confirms that macro-regime modulation of a second process (the
C8 structure: Primed macro state x L2 micro state) is within the
established methodological family, i.e. C8 is not exotic — which
cuts both ways: established methods have known failure modes
(Hansen 1992 inference, small-regime-sample noise).
