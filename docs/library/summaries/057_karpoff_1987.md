# 057 — The Relation Between Price Changes and Trading Volume: A Survey (Karpoff, 1987)

**Venue:** Journal of Finance | **Category:** Microstructure & order flow
**Status:** Synthesized from LLM internal knowledge (Paywalled paper; catalogue notes a citation cross-check).

## Summary
The classic survey of the volume-price relation: volume correlates
positively with the absolute price change (and with volatility), a
fact robust across markets and decades. Karpoff organizes the
theories (information arrival, mixture-of-distributions,
asymmetric-information models) and the empirical regularities,
including the well-known positive volume-volatility relation that
the project's panel also shows.

## The math that matters
Central regularity: $\text{Cov}(|\Delta P_t|, V_t) > 0$; the
mixture-of-distributions hypothesis models both as driven by a
common information-arrival intensity:
$\Delta P_t, V_t \mid \theta_t$ jointly determined by a
directing process $\theta_t$.

## Why it matters for the L2 project
The volume-|return| relation is the OHLCV shadow of the L2
quantity OFI measures directly: volume flows through the book.
Any C8 micro feature built from volume must be checked against
the book-based version to avoid re-measuring the same factor
under a new name (the compression lesson again).
