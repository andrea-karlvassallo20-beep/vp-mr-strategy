# 059 — A Stochastic Partial Differential Equation Model for Limit Order Book Dynamics (2019)

**Venue:** SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics | **Category:** Price impact & execution
**Status:** Synthesized from LLM internal knowledge (Paywalled paper; RESEARCH LEAD per catalogue; authors to verify).

## Summary
Continuum limit of the LOB: the density of resting orders on the
price axis evolves by a SPDE with stochastic arrival/removal
terms; the price moves when the density at the touch is depleted.
Connects the microscopic event process to a tractable
density-valued SDE/SPDE, giving analytic handles on price impact
and queue depletion dynamics.

## The math that matters
Density $u(t, x)$ evolving as
$du = \mathcal{L}u\,dt + \sigma\, dW_t$-type SPDE with
state-dependent boundary flux at the touch; mid-price defined by
the hitting condition $u(t, m_t) \to 0$; impact of a market order
of size $q$ scales with the local density — the mechanistic
source of the square-root law.

## Why it matters for the L2 project
The research lead for the L2 family: the SPDE view predicts
specific functional forms (depth-dependent impact, queue-exhaustion
timing) that can be tested on the Tardis incremental book data —
falsifiable microstructure, unlike the OHLCV compression family.
