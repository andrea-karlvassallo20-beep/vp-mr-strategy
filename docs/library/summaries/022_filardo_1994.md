# 022 — Business Cycle Phases and Their Transitional Dynamics (Filardo, 1994)

**Venue:** Journal of Business & Economic Statistics | **Category:** Regime switching & state-state
**Status:** Synthesized from LLM internal knowledge (Paywalled paper).

## Summary
Extends Hamilton's fixed transition matrix to **time-varying
transition probabilities** driven by observable leading indicators
(the composite index of leading indicators). The probability of
regime change becomes a logistic function of data, so the model can
say "a recession is likely to begin" before the level data show it.
Applied to US business cycles, it beats fixed-transition
specifications in predicting phase transitions.

## The math that matters
$p_t = \Pr(s_t = 1 \mid s_{t-1} = 1, z_t) =
\Lambda(\alpha_0 + \alpha_1 z_t) = 1/(1+e^{-(\alpha_0+\alpha_1 z_t)})$
with $z_t$ the leading indicator; the rest is the Hamilton filter
with $p_{ij,t}$ in place of constants.

## Why it matters for the L2 project
C8 is precisely a time-varying-transition idea: the macro state
(C7 Primed) may change the probability that the L2 micro state
transitions or matters. Filardo is the classical license for
letting one observed state modulate another — with the same
discipline: the modulation must be pre-registered, not discovered
on the test set.
