Salient.

The Salient Market Hypothesis: a theoretical framework for identifying, in advance, the significant events in a market — a direct replacement for Elliott Wave that keeps what it got right, discards what it got wrong, and adds the dimension it never had: time.

State instead of count. Clock instead of prophecy. Calibration instead of belief. Named by Andrea; built, measured, and scored in public.

the diagnosis

What Elliott Wave got right, and the dimension it never had

RIGHT, and now measured: impulse/correction alternation (horizon-structured autocorrelation); scale self-similarity (in the variance process, not price geometry); crowd footprints (herding, effort-vs-result).

WRONG: the fixed 5-3 cage (any impulse can be counted as a three); Fibonacci targets (no evidence in the canon); unfalsifiability (any count can be recounted).

NEVER HAD: time. Elliott says where, never when. This thesis makes time a first-class dimension — the clock IS the theory.

the central theorem

The certainty–timing–direction triangle

A public rule that is certain, timed, AND directional cannot exist: anyone who sees it trades it, and the event no longer happens as forecast. So the claims are separated:

  • CERTAINTY lives at the distribution (the six laws)
  • PROBABILITY lives at the event (conditional rates by state)
  • HAZARD lives at the timing (the clock)

The observer corollary: the only forecast that cannot be priced away is the private one — and the only private edge this programme can build is a forward-scored calibration record. It cannot move the market, cannot be flattered, and compounds.

the axioms

The six unconditional laws

  • 1. Volatility persists, then mean-reverts — the clock itself.
  • 2. Tails are heavy — thresholds live in sigma units, never points.
  • 3. Autocorrelation has horizon structure — the directional prior, only inside trend states.
  • 4. Impact is concave in volume — a significant event must be confirmed by volume (effort vs result).
  • 5. Events self-excite — hazard is state-dependent, never constant.
  • 6. Every edge decays — every rule carries a forward score and a decay watch.

Not on the list, provably: the direction of the next impulse at a chosen time.

the read

AVFI v4 — what it is, and what it is not

AVFI v4 is the READ: causal stretch-z with confirmed tops and bottoms (deep stretch + bull/bear confirmation within 3 bars) — the points of significant change, marked on every chart of the universe (185 series on the charts page).

Measured: v4 confirmed bottoms on indexes are genuine change points (+4.1% median forward, 76.9% positive); on crypto they read negative — honest, flagged. The v4 confirmation premium exists pooled but the concentrated book does not compound better (the portfolio referee, finding 5 of section 16).

The decisive test (charter section 17): every AVFI configuration ALONE reads at or below climatology (the confirmed-bottom cell reads 0.399 vs climatology 0.435 — after a confirmed bottom, the move already happened). But INSIDE the clock, stretch adds hazard: compression AND stretch reads 0.657 [0.648, 0.665] vs the clock alone 0.635 — disjoint bands, design-sample, flagged. AVFI is the READ that colors the armed clock: never a trigger, never an authorizer.

v4 colors regimes; it never authorizes a trade. No out-of-fold hazard gain was ever measured — that verdict stands.

the long rule

The only measured long edge: the crash-zone cross

Enter ONCE per crash episode at the cross into the moderate weekly crash (−5% to −10% — a guideline, not a rule, and the depth gradient is NOT monotone: the nuclear-red crash reads lower, folklore). The verdicts grade honestly: LONG_CANDIDATE vs LONG_CANDIDATE_FLAGGED (negative cell or stress region — flagged, never excluded).

Measured: 206 episode trades, median net 0.0169 over 4 weeks, 0.573 positive, and the dual-split gate PASSES in both worse windows, net of costs. The OOS holdout (11 new global indexes) replicates: +3.07% median, 71.4% positive.

the portfolio

the honest close

State instead of count, clock instead of prophecy, calibration instead of belief

The inevitable rules exist: the six laws, found not invented. What they produce is a distribution, and the anticipated event appears inside it with a probability stated in advance and a clock you can score. Less romantic than waves — and it has one property no wave theory ever had: it can be wrong in public, which is the only way anything in markets can ever be right.
